    A LITTLE ABOUT ME

    A person behind the projects.

    An engineering student, a drummer, a cat person.
    Always wondering: “Could I make that?”

    I study Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. Circuits, code and experiments help me answer questions. Music, pets and small everyday moments keep giving me new ones.

    Sometimes I want to make a comfortable pet collar. Sometimes it is a road that unfolds with a song, or a tiny tool that makes daily life easier. Project S is where I collect those ideas and work out how to bring them to life.
