01

Wireless charging
for a pet collar

FINAL-YEAR PROJECT · SIMULATION

LTspice modelling of a series-compensated inductive link with bounded PI frequency control. 50 simulation cases across 11 iterations.

What was tested

5 V ±5% during tested coupling and load changes under ideal drive and feedback assumptions. Approximate model efficiency: 61% nominal, 35% with weak coupling and light load. These are simulation findings, not a completed charging product.

02

AutoProbe-GPU

GEARS · NCSU · SUMMER 2026

LLM-assisted GPU exploration on an RTX 4060, with compilation, SASS inspection and hardware validation.

Result & contribution

A generated dependent-load probe showed a 2.12% latency increase between 128 B and 16 KiB node spacing across three sweeps. No unverified cache or TLB mechanism is claimed.

03

Power on the bench

FOUR-PERSON DESIGN PROJECT

Contributed to a 50 W two-switch forward converter through PCB assembly, output adjustment and simulation-to-bench analysis.

Bench evidence

Approximately 10 V output and 110 mV peak-to-peak ripple with DC input, Type I control and a 2.2-ohm load; within the 200 mV requirement.

Practice beyond the lab
